LunaLight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LunaLight is a companion app for a personal, DIY hardware lamp project that syncs its lighting schedule to the moon's phases. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it's handled.

Location

When you tap "Sync Time & Location," LunaLight reads your phone's current GPS coordinates one time and sends them directly to your own lamp over Bluetooth, so it can calculate accurate moonrise and moonset times for where you live. LunaLight does not track your location in the background, does not store your location history, and does not send your location to any server. It stays on your phone and your device.

Bluetooth

LunaLight connects over Bluetooth Low Energy directly to your own lamp hardware to read and change its settings (schedule, brightness, manual on/off, and so on). This connection is local, device-to-device, and does not involve any external server.

Journal Content ("Your Signal In The Noise")

If you use the journaling feature, your written answers and mood ratings are stored only on your own device's local storage. LunaLight does not upload, transmit, or have any server-side access to this content.

Optional Email Archive

The "Archive Signal" feature lets you export your journal entries by email. This is entirely user-initiated: tapping it opens your phone's own Mail app with the content already filled in, addressed to an email address you choose. LunaLight does not send this email itself, does not have its own mail server, and does not see or store a copy of what gets sent. You control whether that message actually gets sent.

What LunaLight Does Not Do

  • No account creation or login is required.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers are used.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No data is stored on any server operated by the developer.

Data Deletion

You can delete your journal entries at any time from within the app's Archive feature. Uninstalling LunaLight removes all locally stored data, since nothing is retained elsewhere.

Children's Privacy

LunaLight is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
